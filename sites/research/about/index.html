About

Independent research and development.

Emtypyie & Co. is not attached to a university department or a product line. We pick questions because they are open, follow them at whatever pace the work demands, and publish the result even when it disproves what we set out to show.

We would rather be wrong in public than quiet in private.

What we work on

Three areas, chosen because they keep intersecting in practice: machine learning, aerospace, and embedded systems. A model is only as useful as the constrained hardware it eventually runs on, and a spacecraft is only as reliable as the firmware controlling it — so the lab tends to work across all three rather than picking one.

How we work

Every researcher owns their line of work end to end — there is no separate team that writes up someone else's results. Papers go up on this site before they go anywhere else, and stay here permanently, revisions and all.

Funding

The lab is self-funded. We take on select applied contracts in aerospace and embedded engineering to cover costs, and keep that work clearly separated from open research.

Getting in touch

Reach any researcher directly from their page under Team, or write to hello@emtypyie.in for general questions.
