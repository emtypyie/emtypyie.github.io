Privacy.

What this site collects, and what it doesn't.

last updated september 2026

This policy covers research.emtypyie.in. We keep the data we collect to what the site needs to run, and nothing more.

What we collect

  • Basic access logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) kept for security and traffic analysis.
  • Any information you send us directly, such as through email to a researcher or to hello@emtypyie.in.
  • No account creation or sign-in is required to use this site, so we do not collect login credentials.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent visitor data to third parties.
  • We don't run third-party advertising trackers on this site.
  • We don't build advertising profiles from your activity here.

Cookies

This site does not set tracking or advertising cookies. Any cookies used are strictly functional, such as remembering a display preference.

Third-party links

Papers, personal researcher sites, and outbound links are outside our control. Their own privacy practices apply once you leave research.emtypyie.in.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@emtypyie.in.
